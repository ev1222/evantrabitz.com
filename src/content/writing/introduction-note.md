Hello! I am Evan Trabitz, an AI Engineer. Most engineers’ personal websites are a showcase of technical projects they’ve worked on and a summary of their skillsets, or perhaps of their research. No doubt there will be projects and skillsets, but one of my most important and ongoing projects is my writing: I love to write, about as long as I’ve loved to work on software. 

As such, it felt only natural to have my personal website host my professional-facing pieces pertinent to my field. Readers can expect discussion of AI (what else) as it relates to my work, the world, and itself. I have a background in, and deep passion for, philosophy, and given how critical a piece of AI development philosophy has become, I feel it necessary to weigh in. I am also interested in physics, economics, finance, and more, which should make for occasional tangents.
